Scoring rules (apply strictly against structured requirements above):

role_relevance (0–100):
- 85–100: Title and description clearly match a PRIMARY role family; hands-on data/analytics/AI engineering work is central.
- 60–84: Good match to a SECONDARY role family, or primary family with mixed responsibilities.
- 40–59: Partial overlap (e.g. adjacent role, heavy management, or platform ops with little analytics work).
- 0–39: Matches AVOID list, generic SWE/IT, or little connection to stated role families despite title keyword.

mission_alignment (0–100):
- 85–100: Strong fit with "excited about" sectors or clear social/climate/humanitarian impact in description.
- 50–84: Neutral sector or mission mentioned but not central.
- 21–49: Weak mission fit; commercial B2B with no stated impact.
- 0–20: Any listed mission deal-breaker, or industry explicitly opposed in requirements.

candidate_fit (0–100):
- Weight: seniority match (target/acceptable vs too_junior/too_senior), core skills vs job requirements, years/trajectory vs background summary, organization preferences.
- 85–100: Strong seniority + stack match; requirements align with core strengths.
- 60–84: Reasonable fit with some gaps noted in risks_or_gaps.
- 40–59: Significant seniority or skills mismatch.
- 0–39: Clearly wrong level, wrong stack, or conflicts with organization/deal-breaker preferences.

Fit checks — read the FULL posting (title, location line, description) and decide each boolean strictly. The candidate is an EU citizen based in the EU (CET) who can only work fully remote:

remote_ok (boolean):
- true only if the job can be done fully remote. On-site or hybrid requirements (any required office days, "X days per week in office", relocation) → false, unless a fully-remote alternative is explicitly offered.
- Plain "Remote" or no stated location: true (unspecified is acceptable per requirements).

eu_hire_ok (boolean):
- true if the employer can hire someone based in the EU: hire region includes the EU/Europe or an EU country, the role is global/work-from-anywhere, or no hire region is stated.
- false if the posting restricts hiring to a non-EU region (e.g. "Remote — US only", "United States (Remote)", US city with no remote option, "must be based in the US/UK/Canada"), requires US work authorization, or requires relocation outside the EU.
- Watch for compensation/benefits/eligibility notes written only for US employees combined with US-only language.

timezone_ok (boolean):
- true if working hours are compatible with European business hours (roughly 09:00–17:00 CET): European/EMEA hours, flexible/async hours, global team, or nothing stated.
- false if the role requires US business hours or substantial US-timezone overlap (e.g. "EST/PST working hours", "core hours 9–5 Eastern", "must overlap 6+ hours with US team").

seniority_ok (boolean):
- true if the required level matches the target/acceptable seniority in the requirements (mid-level; junior acceptable).
- false if the role is clearly above it (senior/staff/principal/lead/head/management, or requires far more years of experience than the candidate has) or below it (internship/graduate scheme).

fit_reasons: up to 3 short strings explaining any false fit-check boolean (empty array when all true).

Other fields:
- extracted_salary: short string if comp stated, else null. Compare to minimum in requirements when scoring risks.
- top_requirements: up to 5 short strings from the posting.
- risks_or_gaps: up to 5 short strings (seniority, visa, hybrid, comp below minimum, stack gaps, deal-breakers).
- one_line_summary: single punchy line for the digest.

Be conservative on scores when information is missing and explain in risks_or_gaps — but for the fit-check booleans, missing information means "no restriction stated" (see each rule above).
